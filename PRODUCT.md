# Sha Silvers — product context

**Register:** product

A Coimbatore manufacturer of 925 sterling silverware selling direct. Not a
jewellery house: the shop is thalis, plates, spoons, tumblers, diyas, idols
and gifting. Jewellery is one occasional category, deliberately de-emphasised
in the nav.

## Users

Two, and they want opposite things from the same page.

**The occasion buyer.** Buying a wedding or housewarming gift, often on a
phone, often at night, usually deciding between three articles by weight and
price. Does not know silver. Needs the total, the delivery date and the
hallmark stated without hunting. Abandons at the first unexpected cost.

**The repeat household buyer.** Knows the rate, knows what 218 g means, is
checking whether today's making charge is fair. Wants density: more articles
per screen, the metal/making split visible, no marketing between them and the
grid.

Neither is browsing for pleasure. The interface should get out of the way.

## What the product must prove

Silver is bought on trust in three facts, and the interface exists to state
them early and repeat them without fuss:

1. **What it weighs.** Nett grams, with tolerance.
2. **What the metal costs today.** Weight x rate, shown as its own line.
3. **What we charge to make it.** Making charge, named, never buried.

A price with no breakdown reads as a markup. The breakdown is the product.

## Tone

Plain, specific, unhurried. States facts; does not sell. "Raised from a single
sheet, spun and polished in-house" rather than "crafted with passion". Numbers
over adjectives. No urgency, no scarcity, no countdowns.

## Anti-references

- Gold-and-maroon Indian jewellery retail. The category reflex; we are a
  workshop, not a showroom.
- Marketplace density with badges, ribbons, "only 2 left", star ratings.
- Luxury-minimal fashion e-commerce where the product is a mood and the price
  is hidden until checkout.
- Any interface that treats the price breakdown as fine print.

## Strategic principles

- **Costs up front.** Shipping, GST and making charge appear on the tile, the
  product page and the bag. Nothing new appears at payment.
- **Restraint means density, not emptiness.** More product per screen, fewer
  decorations. Whitespace is not the goal; clarity is.
- **Search and category nav are permanent controls**, not icons behind a menu.
- **Three clicks to paid**, two from any grid via quick-add.
- **Colour carries meaning only.** Money, promises, state and structure. Never
  decoration for its own sake.
